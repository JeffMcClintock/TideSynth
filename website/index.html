TIDE

A modular synth that lives inside your DAW.

TIDE is a patching environment that runs as a plugin. You build the instrument in the same window you play it in — there is no separate application, nothing to export, and no build step between an idea and a sound. Your DAW supplies the audio and the MIDI; you supply the patch.

It is a cut-down SynthEdit: the same structure view, the same modules, reduced to what makes sense inside a host.

Status: in development. There is nothing to download yet. The first release will do one thing — load in a DAW, let you wire an oscillator and an envelope to an output, play it from your keyboard, and still be there when you reopen the project.

Price

TIDE is free. Not free-for-now, not free-with-a-paid-tier: no trial period, no licence key, no unlock, and no feature held back for a version that costs money.

It is funded by donations. If TIDE turns out to be useful to you, there is a link below — and that is the only place you will be asked. Nothing in the plugin will interrupt you to bring it up.

Source

TIDE is open source, under the ISC licence — the same terms as the GMPI libraries it builds on. The code, the plan and the working notes are all at github.com/JeffMcClintock/TideSynth, and the project is developed in the open, decisions and dead ends included.

TIDE is a SynthEdit project.
